# 1. Design the homepage in Claude Design

Paste this into Claude Design, with the client folder (brief, brand assets, photos) attached. It works
through the homepage with you, asks where a decision is genuinely needed, and stops for your review
before handing to Claude Code (prompt 2).

---

You are designing the homepage for this client. **Work through it with me and ask before you assume.**
The brief carries the strategy, audience, voice and page notes, so design to it, do not re-do strategy.

## Start
1. Read the client brief and the brand assets (logo files, photos).
2. **Confirm you have what you need, and ask me for anything missing or ambiguous** before you design:
   the logo files (never redraw the mark from a screenshot), any open scope question in the brief, and
   any brand colour the brief uses that fails WCAG AA (flag it and propose how to handle it). Ask one
   question at a time; do not stall on things you can sensibly default from the brief.

## Design
- Design the **homepage (desktop)** to the brand palette and fonts exactly, the signature accent
  dominant, modernised within the palette; match the voice to the visual (the brief's tone section);
  follow the homepage notes; put the primary call to action in the header, the body and the foot, with
  tap-to-call on mobile.
- **Avoid the AI look:** intentional asymmetry and spatial rhythm, brand-specific colour, an unexpected
  hierarchy, not centred and uniform with even padding. Honour the brief's "what to avoid".
- Keep **WCAG 2.2 AA** contrast; where a bright accent fails on white, use it as a field or on a dark
  ground, not as text or a thin mark on white.

## Finish, and stop
- Show me the homepage and **tell me the judgment calls you made** (how you handled any contrast
  conflict, anywhere you departed from the brief and why). **Stop for my review.**
- Once I am happy, use **"transfer to Claude Code"** so we build the Figma design system with prompt 2.
  **Do not transfer before I approve.**
