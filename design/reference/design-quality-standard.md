# Design Quality Standard

The goal is a distinctive, usable website that serves the approved brief and remains consistent across pages.

## Design with a clear idea

Every direction needs:

- a one-sentence thesis
- an intended audience response
- a signature element that supports the thesis
- a clear hierarchy and conversion path
- concrete do and do not guidance

Avoid vague direction such as `modern and clean`. Describe observable choices and how those choices affect the design.

## Avoid generic AI patterns

Do not default to:

- warm cream, a serif typeface and terracotta accents
- near-black with acid green
- default blue and generic grey palettes
- every section centred and symmetrical
- repeated 50/50 split layouts
- uniform spacing across every section
- identical rounded cards for unrelated content
- predictable hierarchy where every heading and action has the same treatment
- decorative motion without a user or communication purpose

Difference must serve the brief. Do not introduce asymmetry or novelty only to appear different.

## Use content as design material

- Use approved or realistic content instead of filler.
- Test long headings, long labels and extra list items.
- Define behaviour for missing images, empty states and errors.
- Record content-length assumptions.
- Keep the main action easy to understand.

## Responsive design

- Design desktop, tablet and mobile behaviour.
- Record what changes at each size, including columns, order, scale and hidden elements.
- Use Auto Layout and intentional resizing.
- Preserve content priority when layouts stack or reorder.

## Accessibility at design time

- Check colour contrast against WCAG 2.2 AA.
- Provide visible focus states.
- Record accessible names and label intent.
- Do not rely on colour alone to communicate state.
- Record reduced-motion behaviour.
- Treat automated checks as assistance. They do not certify accessibility.

## Imagery, icons and motion

- Define subject matter, composition, crop and treatment for imagery.
- Use one coherent icon family.
- Record image aspect ratios and fallback behaviour.
- Use motion only when it communicates state, hierarchy or progress.
- Provide a reduced-motion alternative.

## Final quality question

Before approval, ask:

> Could this design belong to another client if the logo and colours were changed?

If the answer is yes, strengthen the thesis, composition, content treatment or signature element before continuing.
