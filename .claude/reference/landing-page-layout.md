# Landing page layout

How a paid landing page is laid out on any builder. Read it before building or restyling one, with
the project's design source. Written from Craig's reviews of the Aatach landing pages, 27 November
2026. Every rule here came from a fault he found on a built page.

## The page

- **The site's own home page is the design reference.** Match its banner, image rows, buttons,
  section bands and type. Where a part is built into the theme and cannot be placed on a page,
  rebuild its look with the builder's own blocks. Never add a new colour or font.
- **Published and set to noindex,** never a draft, unless the build's rules say otherwise.
  Out of the menu and the sitemap.

## Banner

- **Four things and nothing else.** The H1, a subheading of one short sentence, three short
  bullet points, and two buttons. A full-width photo behind them with a dark overlay.
- **Keep the text short.** If the copy file gives the banner more than that (a trust paragraph, a
  second sentence), stop and ask ZilvaEdge for the copy; do not squeeze it in.
- **No form in the banner.**

## The form

- **One form per page, in the quote section at the foot,** above the FAQs. Never in the banner or
  the second row.
- **Every Get a quote button links to the quote section** (anchor `quote`), including the banner and
  the mobile sticky bar. Call buttons are `tel:` links.
- **The quote section** is styled like the home page's own quote section, never a black band.

## Cards

- **Equal-sized cards that fill their rows.** Three or four across on desktop, six as two rows of
  three. Never five, and never one card stretched full width under a row of others. If the copy
  gives five, ask ZilvaEdge to cut one.
- **Cards stack to one column on a phone.**

## Call-to-action rows

- **One mid-page row at most, plus the quote section at the foot.** A short heading and the button,
  with no description line.
- **Never turn a content section into a call-to-action row.** A section's own text stays a normal
  section. A Get a quote button that the copy places under a section is a plain button there, not a
  band.
- **Never two coloured bands close together.** Separate them with at least one normal section.

## Lists and labels

- **A bold label at the start of a list stays a label.** "Materials we clean" or "Marks we remove"
  is not a heading; never promote it to H2 or H3.
- **Two short lists that belong together sit side by side on desktop,** in two columns, and stack on
  a phone.

## Images and text rows

- **Text sections get an image,** as image-and-text rows alternating left and right like the home
  page. Reuse photos already in the media library; check each for people, house numbers, street
  signs, number plates and brand signs before use.
- **Before and after pairs** sit side by side at a useful size, captioned underneath.

## FAQs

- **An accordion, closed on load,** styled like the home page's. Build every question in the copy
  file; a section of three or fewer questions goes back to ZilvaEdge for more.

## Before handing a page over

Check it on desktop and a phone, logged out, against every heading above, and say which rule a page
departs from and why.
